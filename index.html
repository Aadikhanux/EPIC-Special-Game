HOW TO PLAY

1

Frame Your Shot

Raise both hands with index fingers extended. The box between your fingers creates your camera frame.

2

Pinch to Lock & Snap

Pinch index & thumb on both hands simultaneously to start the 3-second countdown and flash!

3

Solve the 3×3 Puzzle

Pinch with one hand (or click & drag with mouse/touch) to move pieces. Drop near the slot to snap in.

4

Make a Fist to Save

Hold a closed fist for 1s to shatter the puzzle into your color polaroid strip, or click "Save & Continue".

💡

Recapture Crop

Want to re-take the photo? Click the RECAPTURE CROP button anytime or press the R key.
